# Pet Astronaut

**Phrase:** "Out of This World"
**Aspect ratio:** 1:1
**Theme:** space / pets

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration set on a deeply textured
black chalkboard background, lit by a single warm top-down spotlight
that fades into shadow at the corners.

The composition centers on a happy mixed-breed dog, tongue out, fur
catching warm rim light, photographed at eye level with crisp detail
in the muzzle and eyes. The head is rendered in high-resolution
photorealistic detail — visible fur strands, wet-nose highlight,
bright catchlights — and integrated as a seamless cutout onto a body
drawn entirely in fine white chalk outlines. The chalk body must look
genuinely hand-drawn: slight smudges, broken-line shading, faint chalk
dust along the edges.

The chalk body is depicted floating in a whimsical spacesuit, arms
out slightly as if drifting weightless, helmet ring sketched in chalk
around the neck, with line-weight variation suggesting motion.

Surrounding the figure, the chalkboard is decorated with hand-drawn
white chalk motifs: sparkling constellations connected by faint lines,
two ringed planets in opposite corners, scattered shooting stars, and
small dog paw prints trailing across the lower edge. Decorations feel
sketched, not printed. Leave negative space.

Elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography appears in the scene,
reading: "Out of This World".

Style notes: mixed-media collage, photo-bashing, chalkboard art. Pure
white chalk only. The warm spotlight should tint both the realistic
head and the chalk work so they read as one image.
