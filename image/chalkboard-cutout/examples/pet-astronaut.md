# The Pet Astronaut

**Phrase:** "Out of This World"
**Theme:** space / pets

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration on a dark, textured black chalkboard background illuminated by a subtle top spotlight. The main character features a high-quality, photorealistic cutout head of a happy mixed-breed dog, seamlessly attached to a body drawn entirely in minimalist white chalk outlines. The chalk body is depicted floating in a whimsical spacesuit. The surrounding chalkboard is decorated with white chalk-drawn elements such as sparkling constellations, planets with rings, and small dog paw prints. Included in the scene is elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography that reads "Out of This World".
