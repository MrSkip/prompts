# Celestial Dragon

**Phrase:** "Celestial Flame"
**Aspect ratio:** 1:1
**Theme:** mythical / cosmic

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration set on a deeply textured
black chalkboard background, lit by a single warm top-down spotlight
that fades into shadow at the corners.

The composition centers on a vibrant blue iguana head, scales catching
sharp specular highlights, one slit pupil glinting, photographed in
crisp macro detail. The head is rendered in high-resolution
photorealistic detail — visible scale texture, dewlap fold, catchlight
in the eye — and integrated as a seamless cutout onto a magnificent
dragon body drawn entirely in fine white chalk outlines. The chalk
body must look genuinely hand-drawn: smudges, broken-line shading,
cross-hatching on the wing membranes, chalk dust along the edges.

The chalk dragon body features large outstretched wings with sketched
membrane veining, a long sinuous tail curling beneath, and chalk-dust
breath spiraling from the mouth as if exhaling embers.

Surrounding the figure, the chalkboard is decorated with hand-drawn
white chalk motifs: stylized flame tongues at the lower corners, two
comets with trailing sparks, a slim crescent moon in the upper left,
and scattered ember specks. Leave negative space.

Elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography appears in the scene,
reading: "Celestial Flame".

Style notes: mixed-media collage, photo-bashing, chalkboard art. Pure
white chalk only. Unified warm spotlight.
