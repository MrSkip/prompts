# The Celestial Dragon

**Phrase:** "Celestial Flame"
**Theme:** mythical / cosmic

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration on a dark, textured black chalkboard background illuminated by a subtle top spotlight. The main character features a high-quality, photorealistic cutout head of a vibrant blue iguana, seamlessly attached to a magnificent dragon body drawn entirely in minimalist white chalk outlines. The chalk dragon body features large wings and is breathing chalk-dust fire. The surrounding chalkboard is decorated with white chalk-drawn elements such as flames, comets, and a crescent moon. Included in the scene is elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography that reads "Celestial Flame".
