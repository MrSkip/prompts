# Dune — Fremen Litany

**Phrase:** "I must not fear. Fear is the mind-killer."
**Aspect ratio:** 2:3 (poster)
**Theme:** Dune / Frank Herbert

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration set on a deeply textured
black chalkboard background, lit by a single warm top-down spotlight
that fades into shadow at the corners.

The composition centers on a young woman with piercing blue-within-blue
Ibad eyes, short cropped sand-colored hair, a slight desert tan, and a
contemplative expression as if steadying herself before battle.
Photographed in dramatic side-light with warm amber catching one side
of the face and cool shadow on the other. The head is rendered in
high-resolution photorealistic detail — visible skin texture, fine
strands of hair, sharp catchlights in the unnatural blue eyes — and
integrated as a seamless cutout onto a body drawn entirely in fine
white chalk outlines. The chalk body must look genuinely hand-drawn:
smudges, broken-line shading, cross-hatching along the stillsuit
panels, chalk dust along the edges.

The chalk body is standing in three-quarter profile wearing a fitted
Fremen stillsuit with visible recycler tubing across the torso,
nose-plugs hanging unused at the collar, one hand resting on the hilt
of a sheathed crysknife at the hip.

Surrounding the figure, the chalkboard is decorated with hand-drawn
white chalk motifs: a massive sandworm arcing across the lower third
of the board with faint rings of teeth visible at the mouth, the twin
crescent moons of Arrakis in the upper right, distant dune ridges
receding into the horizon, scattered chalk "spice" specks drifting
like dust motes, and a small Atreides hawk sigil in the lower-left
corner. Decorations feel sketched, not printed. Leave negative space.

Elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography appears in the scene,
reading: "I must not fear. Fear is the mind-killer."

Style notes: mixed-media collage, photo-bashing, chalkboard art. Pure
white chalk only — no color bleed. Unified warm spotlight tinting both
the realistic head and the chalk work.

--ar 2:3 --style raw --no color, painted, 3d render

## Notes

- `--ar 2:3` gives vertical poster room for the sandworm arc.
- `--no color, painted, 3d render` because Dune themes pull the model
  toward sci-fi 3D renders by default.
- The "blue-within-blue" eye detail is what sells the Fremen identity —
  don't drop it.
