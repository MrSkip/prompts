# Dune — The Spice Must Flow

**Phrase:** "The Spice must Flow"
**Theme:** Dune / Frank Herbert

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration on a dark, textured black chalkboard background illuminated by a subtle top spotlight. The main character features a high-quality, photorealistic cutout head of a young man, seamlessly attached to a body drawn entirely in minimalist white chalk outlines. The chalk body is depicted standing confidently in a Fremen stillsuit, holding a glowing paracompass. The surrounding chalkboard is decorated with white chalk-drawn elements such as a massive sandworm rising from the dunes, the twin crescent moons of Arrakis, a distant chalk silhouette of Arrakeen city, an ornithopter in flight, glowing spice crystals, and the Atreides hawk sigil. Included in the scene is elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography that reads "The Spice must Flow".
