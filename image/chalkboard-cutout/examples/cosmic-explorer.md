# Cosmic Explorer

**Phrase:** "Explore Beyond"
**Aspect ratio:** 1:1
**Theme:** space / aspiration

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration set on a deeply textured
black chalkboard background, lit by a single warm top-down spotlight
that fades into shadow at the corners.

The composition centers on a smiling young woman in her mid-twenties,
looking upward with quiet wonder, lit softly from above with a warm
amber glow catching the cheekbones. The head is rendered in
high-resolution photorealistic detail — visible skin texture,
individual eyelashes, catchlights in the eyes — and integrated as a
seamless cutout onto a body drawn entirely in fine white chalk
outlines. The chalk body must look genuinely hand-drawn: slight
smudges, broken-line shading, chalk dust along the edges.

The chalk body is standing in three-quarter profile, leaning forward
slightly with one chalk hand resting on the barrel of a large
chalk-drawn telescope angled up toward the corner of the frame.

Surrounding the figure, the chalkboard is decorated with hand-drawn
white chalk motifs: shooting stars with motion trails, distant planets
of varying sizes, scattered stardust like fine chalk-dust speckles,
and a faint constellation outline overhead. Leave negative space.

Elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography appears in the scene,
reading: "Explore Beyond".

Style notes: mixed-media collage, photo-bashing, chalkboard art. Pure
white chalk only. Unified warm spotlight across head and chalk work.
