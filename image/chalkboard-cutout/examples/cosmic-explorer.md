# The Cosmic Explorer

**Phrase:** "Explore Beyond"
**Theme:** space / aspiration

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration on a dark, textured black chalkboard background illuminated by a subtle top spotlight. The main character features a high-quality, photorealistic cutout head of a smiling young woman, seamlessly attached to a body drawn entirely in minimalist white chalk outlines. The chalk body is depicted standing and looking up through a large telescope. The surrounding chalkboard is decorated with white chalk-drawn elements such as shooting stars, distant planets, and scattered stardust. Included in the scene is elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography that reads "Explore Beyond".
