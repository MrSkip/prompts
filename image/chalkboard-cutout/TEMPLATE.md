# Chalkboard Cutout — Template

Mixed-media illustration: photoreal head fused with a chalk-drawn body on a
black chalkboard background. The head can be either a text-described subject
(text-to-image) or an uploaded photograph (image-conditioned) — in the latter
case keep the SUBJECT description minimal so the prompt doesn't fight the
uploaded face.

## Placeholders

- `[SUBJECT]` — e.g. smiling golden retriever / happy toddler / young man.
- `[ACTION/POSE]` — e.g. floating in a spacesuit / holding a glowing
  lantern / looking through a telescope.
- `[DECORATIONS]` — e.g. sparkling constellations, planets with rings,
  shooting stars, and paw prints.
- `[YOUR PHRASE]` — the handwritten chalk text in the scene.

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration on a dark, textured black chalkboard background illuminated by a subtle top spotlight. The main character features a high-quality, photorealistic cutout head of a [SUBJECT, e.g., smiling golden retriever / happy toddler / young man], seamlessly attached to a body drawn entirely in minimalist white chalk outlines. The chalk body is depicted as [ACTION/POSE, e.g., floating in a spacesuit / holding a glowing lantern / looking through a telescope]. The surrounding chalkboard is decorated with white chalk-drawn elements such as [DECORATIONS, e.g., sparkling constellations, planets with rings, shooting stars, and paw prints]. Included in the scene is elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography that reads "[YOUR PHRASE]".
