# Chalkboard Cutout — Template

Mixed-media illustration: a photoreal human or animal head fused with a
chalk-drawn body on a black chalkboard background.

## Placeholders

- `[SUBJECT]` — the head. Include species/age/ethnicity, expression, and
  how the face is lit. Example: *"weathered Fremen warrior with piercing
  blue-within-blue Ibad eyes, sun-cracked skin, side-lit in warm amber."*
- `[POSE]` — what the chalk body is doing, what it's wearing, what it's
  holding. Use specific verbs and named objects.
- `[DECORATIONS]` — chalk-drawn motifs around the figure. 3–5 motifs is
  the sweet spot; more crowds the frame.
- `[PHRASE]` — the handwritten chalk typography in the scene.

## Prompt

A whimsical mixed-media digital illustration set on a deeply textured
black chalkboard background, lit by a single warm top-down spotlight
that fades into shadow at the corners.

The composition centers on [SUBJECT]. The head is rendered in
high-resolution photorealistic detail — visible skin texture, individual
hairs, catchlights in the eyes — and integrated as a seamless cutout
onto a body drawn entirely in fine white chalk outlines. The chalk body
must look genuinely hand-drawn: slight smudges, broken-line shading,
occasional cross-hatching, faint chalk dust along the edges where lines
meet.

The chalk body is [POSE], with line-weight variation suggesting muscle,
fabric, or motion.

Surrounding the figure, the chalkboard is decorated with hand-drawn
white chalk motifs: [DECORATIONS]. Decorations feel sketched, not
printed — uneven strokes, faint chalk dust. Leave negative space; do
not fill every corner.

Elegant, handwritten chalk-style typography appears in the scene,
reading: "[PHRASE]".

Style notes: mixed-media collage, photo-bashing, chalkboard art. Pure
white chalk only — no color bleed into the drawn elements. The warm
spotlight should tint both the realistic head and the chalk work so
they read as one image, not a paste-up.
