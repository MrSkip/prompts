# Tips for Best Results

## Aspect Ratio

Add `--ar 1:1` to the end of your prompt (if using Midjourney) to ensure
you get a clean square shape.

## Style Keywords

If the image generator struggles with the mix of realistic photos and
hand-drawn chalk, try adding trailing keywords like "photo-bashing,"
"collage art style," or "mixed-media scrapbook."

## Image-conditioned (face upload)

If you're using a tool that lets you upload your own photo for the head
(ChatGPT image edit, NanoBanana, Midjourney `--cref`, etc.), keep the
`[SUBJECT]` description generic — e.g. "a young man" — or it will fight
your uploaded face. Don't describe eye color, hair, or features.
